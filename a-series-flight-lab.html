One metre stays one metre—even in overlay.

The nested overlay shares the true-scale view’s metre conversion. It aligns all bases without normalizing height or width, so the A1 and A2 remain visibly tiny beside the A4.

Naming and reliabilityA4 became the V-2. A1 exploded on its stand, all four A3 launches failed, and early A4 prototypes also failed before the successful October 1942 test.

R = v² sin(2θ) / g (flat Earth)
R_curved = R / (1 − v² cos²θ / (g·R_E))
H = v² sin²θ / (2g) · drop = R_curved² / (2R_E)

R_E = 6,371 km (mean Earth radius). Constant gravity, no rotation, no drag.

Model boundaryThe map supplies spatial context, not a reconstructed historical route. The ideal arc uses a constant-gravity, non-rotating spherical-Earth correction; it still omits powered ascent, changing mass, aerodynamic drag, guidance, and winds. Historical rings and points are kept visually separate from the simulated arc.

SOURCES · MODEL LIMITS · HISTORICAL RESPONSIBILITY

Historical responsibilityThe A4/V-2 became a weapon used against populated areas, and its production was tied to forced labour and lethal conditions in the Mittelbau-Dora system. The engineering history cannot responsibly be separated from the victims of production and deployment. Mittelbau-Dora Memorial · Imperial War Museums — V-weapons context.

Rounded values are used for teaching. Internal volumes are approximate; the 3D airframe is a proportional explanatory mesh, not a manufacturing reconstruction. Where sources disagree, a range or “source-dependent” note is shown.
